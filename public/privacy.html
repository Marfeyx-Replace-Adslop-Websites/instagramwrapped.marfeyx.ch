Local Instagram Processing

Instagram Wrapped reads your Instagram export ZIP locally in your browser. Messages, participants, timestamps, reactions, shared links, call metadata, and media references are processed on your device to calculate statistics for the last 365 days.

The ZIP file, parsed chat contents, and computed statistics are not intentionally uploaded by this frontend.

Storage and Retention

Imported Instagram data is kept in browser memory only. It is cleared when the page is refreshed, closed, a new file replaces it. This version does not store Instagram exports, messages, media, or wrapped results in server storage or a database.

Purpose of Processing

  • Parse Instagram exports locally in the browser.
  • Calculate private chat, response time, media, reel, and activity statistics.
